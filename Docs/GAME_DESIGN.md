# Tu Tiên Box — Game Design Vision

## High concept
Tu Tiên Box is a Xianxia World Simulation / God Game inspired by the broad sandbox fantasy of WorldBox. The player is Heavenly Dao: they create and alter the world, observe autonomous life, intervene, and witness consequences and history.

## Player fantasy
“I create the heavens and earth, then watch living beings pursue longevity and the Dao, establish sects, compete for opportunities, rise, fall, and leave legacies.”

## Core loop
CREATE → SIMULATE → OBSERVE → INTERVENE → CONSEQUENCE → HISTORY

## Design pillars
1. **Autonomous world:** NPCs act without constant player commands.
2. **Cultivation as a causal system:** talent, spiritual energy, techniques, resources, time, and risk affect progression.
3. **Inheritance and sect evolution:** characters leave techniques, artifacts, institutions, and conflicts for later generations.
4. **Emergent history:** stories arise from rules and state, not prewritten scripts.
5. **Meaningful intervention:** Heavenly Dao powers alter conditions and create consequences.

## Full-world system targets
- World/environment: terrain, water, climate, day/night, seasons, disasters.
- Spiritual world: spiritual energy, leyline-like spiritual veins, blessed lands, forbidden zones.
- Life: mortals, cultivators, animals, spirit beasts, monsters.
- Life cycle: needs, health, aging, reproduction, death, migration.
- Cultivation: spiritual roots, talent, techniques, realms, bottlenecks, breakthroughs, lifespan, tribulation, ascension.
- Resources: food, water, wood, stone, ore, spirit stones, herbs, beast cores, artifacts.
- Economy: production, gathering, crafting, consumption, trade, scarcity.
- Society: family, village, city, clan, nation.
- Sects/factions: leaders, disciples, territory, resources, recruitment, alliances, rivalry, collapse.
- Conflict: personal combat, beast attacks, territory disputes, sect wars, rebellion, catastrophe.
- Opportunities: secret realms, ruins, cave mansions, inheritance, treasures.
- History: character chronicles, sect histories, wars, disasters, civilization timelines.
- Heavenly Dao: create, destroy, bless, curse, change environment, modify rules, trigger events.
- Presentation: pixel/cell-based world, entity rendering, inspector panels, event feed, history, time controls.

These are long-term targets, not a requirement to implement everything in the first version.

## Distinctive design direction
The game should not merely be “WorldBox with cultivation terminology.” Its potential identity is a world where individual cultivation paths and inheritance can change the fate of sects and civilizations over generations.

## Success criterion
Two worlds with similar initial conditions should be able to develop different histories through understandable causal chains, and the player should be able to inspect why important events occurred.
